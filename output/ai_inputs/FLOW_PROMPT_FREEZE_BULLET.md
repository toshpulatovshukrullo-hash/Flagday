## 1-kadr: zarba (start frame: `K1_zarbadan_oldin.jpg`)
```
Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic football video using the attached image as the first frame. Freeze time and bullet time effect.
0–2 s: normal real-speed match action, the player in the orange shirt and navy shorts runs onto the rolling white ball, broadcast camera from the side.
2–3 s: at the exact moment his right boot is about to hit the ball, time suddenly freezes completely: every player, the ball, blades of grass and specks of turf stop in mid-air like a diorama.
3–6 s: inside the frozen moment the camera makes one fast, smooth 360-degree orbit around the player while pushing in close to the boot and the ball, ending in an extreme macro close-up of the boot touching the ball.
6–8 s: time resumes in super slow motion: the boot strikes the ball, the ball visibly compresses and spins away to the right toward the goal, grass and water droplets spray from the impact.
Same stadium, same kits, same night floodlights, photorealistic, sharp detail, shallow depth of field. The ball always travels to the right, toward the goal. No text, no watermark, no extra players, realistic anatomy, the player's face is never shown in close-up.
```

## 2-kadr: kamera uzoqlashadi, to'p darvozaga uchadi (start frame: `K2_keng_plan_tup_uchmoqda.jpg`)
```
Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic football video using the attached image as the first frame. High wide broadcast angle of a night stadium. The white ball has just been struck from about twenty metres outside the penalty area and flies in a rising curve toward the goal at the upper left of the frame. The camera pulls back and rises smoothly, following the ball in one continuous move, slow motion at first and then accelerating to real speed (speed ramp). Defenders in white turn their heads to watch the ball; the goalkeeper in yellow starts to dive toward the top corner. The ball always moves toward the goal on the upper left, never away from it. Photorealistic broadcast look, sharp detail. No text, no watermark, no extra players.
```

## 3-kadr: darvozabon ulgurmaydi, to'p to'qqizlikka (start frame: `5_darvozabon_ulgurmaydi.jpg`)
```
Create a vertical 9:16 cinematic football video using the attached image as the first frame. Freeze time and bullet time effect, low camera next to the goal post.
0–2 s: super slow motion: the goalkeeper in yellow is diving at full stretch toward the top corner, the ball flies in just beyond his fingertips.
2–3 s: time freezes completely with the ball hanging in the air a few centimetres in front of the goal line, right under the crossbar in the top corner; the goalkeeper's glove is frozen just short of it.
3–6 s: inside the frozen moment the camera makes one smooth 360-degree orbit around the ball and the goalkeeper's glove, pushing in close to the ball, the crossbar and the post.
6–8 s: time resumes: the ball crosses the line into the top corner, the net ripples and bulges outward, and the goalkeeper falls to the grass at real speed.
Photorealistic, night stadium floodlights, sharp detail, shallow depth of field. No text, no watermark, no extra players, realistic anatomy.
```
